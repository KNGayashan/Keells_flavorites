Preparation Steps

Biriyani

Ingredients

500g chicken or mutton (cut into pieces), 2 cups Kohinoor basmati rice (soaked), 1 cup yoghurt2 tomatoes (chopped), 2 onions (sliced & fried golden), 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste Fresh mint & coriander leaves (chopped), 2 tbsp ghee or oil, 1 cup warm milk with a pinch of saffron (optional) Suhana Special Punjabi Crisps (optional, as a side)

  1. Marinate meat – Mix 500g chicken or mutton with 1 cup yoghurt, 2 tbsp ginger-garlic paste, 2 tbsp Biriyani Mix, and salt. Rest 30 minutes.
  2. Cook rice – Boil 2 cups soaked Kohinoor Basmati (Extra-Long Grain) rice with a little salt until 80% done, then drain.
  3. Prepare base – Heat 2 tbsp ghee/oil in a heavy pot, sauté the marinated meat until browned. Add 2 chopped tomatoes and 1 tbsp Biriyani Mix, cook until aromatic.
  4. Layer – Spread half the par-boiled rice over the meat. Top with fried onions, chopped mint, coriander, saffron milk, and sprinkle remaining Biriyani Mix. Add the rest of the rice on top.
  5. Steam (Dum) – Cover tightly and cook on low heat for 20–30 minutes until meat is tender and rice is fluffy.
  6. Let it rest for 10 minutes, gently fluff, and serve hot with raita or curry. Elevate your meal by pairing it with Suhana Special Punjabi Crisps for an extra crunchy side.
